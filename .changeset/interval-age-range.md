Add `IntervalAgeRange`, which shows a span of time as the interval(s) it runs between. A `flavor` picks what goes with the interval names: positions within the intervals (`proportion`), the intervals' bounding ages with the span's own ages in parentheses when they differ (`ages`), both (`both`), or nothing (`none`). `verbose` shows details even when they're implied ("base"/"top" positions, ages that match the intervals').
